# Roadmap

Undavi is a personal project, so there are no dates. Items move up
or down depending on bug reports and what people ask for.

## In Development

- [ ] Static effects that don't depend on audio. (LFO)
- [ ] Particles in the background with custom effects.
- [ ] Reactions (When X, do Y).
- [ ] Modulation grid.

## Planned

The following features are planned for a specific future release version, a general improvement not tied to a specific release, or are being worked on periodically but are unknown when they will be fully implemented.
