# Undavi trademark policy

Undavi's source code is free software under the GNU GPL version 3. This policy is
about something else: the **Undavi name, logo and wordmark** (together, "the
Undavi marks"). The GPL gives you the code, not the marks, so that people can
always tell the official app apart from versions made by others.

In short: **use the code however the GPL allows, but if you change the app, give
it your own name and logo.**

## What you can do without asking

- **Share unmodified Undavi** under the Undavi name, with the logo and the
  watermark exactly as they are. This includes official releases and builds made
  from unmodified source code.
- **Package Undavi for a Linux distribution, Flathub, the AUR or similar**,
  keeping the name, as long as your changes are only what packaging needs
  (build fixes, file paths, dependency patches) and don't change how the app
  works or looks.
- **Talk about Undavi.** Use the name and logo in articles, reviews, videos,
  tutorials and screenshots, and say things like "works with Undavi" or
  "based on Undavi", as long as it's accurate.

## What needs a new name and logo

If you distribute a **modified version** of Undavi (anything beyond the
packaging changes above), you must:

- give it a **different name and logo** that won't be confused with Undavi;
- **remove the Undavi watermark** and logos from it, or replace them with your own;
- **credit the original**, as the GPL additional terms in the NOTICE file
  require: "Based on Undavi by Karson Califf (https://github.com/karsoncaliff/undavi)".

You can say your project is "based on Undavi" or "a fork of Undavi". You can't
call it Undavi, or use a name or logo that looks like it's the official app.

## What's never allowed

- Presenting a modified version, or Undavi itself, as your own original work.
- Suggesting that you or your project is official or endorsed by Undavi when it isn't.
- Using the Undavi marks in your own product names, app store listings, domain
  names or social media accounts in a way that could confuse people.
- Using the Undavi logo for a different product.

## Asking for permission

If you want to do something this policy doesn't cover, or you're not sure,
contact Karson at karsoncaliff@gmail.com. Most reasonable requests are fine.
